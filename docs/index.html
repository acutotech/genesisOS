Genesis Shell

The whole desktop.
Go on, click it.

Panel, launcher, control center, notifications, on-screen display, power dialog and wallpaper are one program we wrote, running on labwc, the compositor Raspberry Pi ships and tests on the Pi 5.

01 The panel

A floating glass panel

The Genesis logo opens the launcher. The dock holds your pinned apps and every running one, with an indicator. The clock opens the calendar and notifications. The status pill opens the control center.

  • Pinned by default: Chromium, VS Code, Files, Terminal, Genesis Settings
  • Click to focus or minimise; right-click to pin, unpin or close
  • Top or bottom of the screen, floating or docked

02 Super Super+Space Super+A

A launcher that fills the screen

Every app over the blurred wallpaper. Start typing and the grid narrows as you type; pick a category chip, or move with the arrow keys and press Enter.

  • Search with type-ahead and category chips
  • Seven columns of 64-pixel icons by default
  • Settings, lock and power one click away

03 Super+C

Control center

Volume and output device, brightness when the screen has a backlight, and tiles for the things you switch often.

  • Wi-Fi with a network list and password entry
  • Bluetooth: paired devices, connect and disconnect
  • Do Not Disturb, screenshot and display settings; Night Light appears once wlsunset is installed

04 Super+N

Notifications that are really the system's

Genesis Shell is the desktop's notification server, so every app's messages arrive as toasts with their buttons, images and urgency, and wait for you behind the clock.

  • Notification center with a calendar beside this session's history
  • Do Not Disturb and an unread badge on the panel
  • Up to four toasts at once; critical ones stay until you dismiss them

05 NodoAI sensor boxes

Plug in a sensor. The desktop notices.

The sensor bar stays hidden until a NodoAI sensor box answers on the I²C bus, then fades in on the right edge with one tile per box. Click a tile for its address, bus and the time it connected.

  • Boxes A to K, scanned every 2 seconds without blocking the shell
  • A notification when a box connects or disconnects
  • A lost probe never flickers the bar: gone means two missed scans
  • It shows which boxes are connected, not their readings; reading a sensor is your program's job

06 Volume and brightness keys

On-screen display

Press a volume or brightness key and a small glass pill shows the level, then gets out of the way. Screenshots go to ~/Pictures/Screenshots and the clipboard with Print, or an area with Shift+Print.

07 Ctrl+Alt+Del or the power key

Power dialog

Lock, log out, restart or shut down from one dialog. Super+L locks straight away, with swaylock dressed in the Genesis colours.

08 Super+← → Alt+Tab

Windows that snap into place

Rounded windows with shadows. Snap and maximise with Super and the arrow keys or by dragging to an edge, switch with a preview of the window you are about to pick, and spread your work over four workspaces.

  • Super+1…4 workspaces, Super+F full screen, Super+Q closes a window
  • Super+T terminal, Super+E files, Super+B browser, Super+I settings
  • Ctrl+Alt+M screen magnifier, Super+= and Super+- to zoom

Design

Frosted glass,
at zero GPU cost.

labwc does not blur what is behind a window. So the shell does not ask it to. Every surface is painted over a blurred, darkened copy of the wallpaper that was prepared once.

Wallpaper
Blurred, darkened copy
Glass tint
Edges and highlight
  1. The wallpaperFive ship with the image: Horizon, Aurora, Midnight, Silverwave and Graphite.
  2. A blurred copy, made oncePrepared when the wallpaper changes, not on every frame. It is the wallpaper that shows through, not the windows behind: the panel reserves its space, and a card simply covers what is under it.
  3. A tint you can setDeep navy at 62% by default. Glass opacity is a slider in Genesis Settings, from 40% to 95%.
  4. Three lines of lightA dark outer line, a light inner line and a brighter highlight along the top. Corners at 16 pixels on the panel, 20 on cards and 12 on windows.

Our own shell, not our own compositor

What you see and touch is the shell, so that is what we wrote. Window management stays with labwc, which Raspberry Pi maintains and tunes for the Pi 5's GPU.

One program, not seven

Panel, launcher, control center, notifications, on-screen display, power dialog, wallpaper and desktop icons are one Python and GTK process per session, so they share one look and one settings file.

Labels you can always read

Each desktop icon label is black or white, whichever contrasts more with the wallpaper right behind it, and is chosen again when the wallpaper changes.

It comes back

If the shell ever stops, the session starts it again, and after five failed starts in a row it falls back to a basic desktop with an explanation. Raspberry Pi's stock desktop stays installed too, selectable on the login screen.

Settings apply live

Wallpaper, accent colour, panel position, glass opacity and screen blanking change the moment you set them in Genesis Settings. No logout.

Inter and JetBrains Mono

The typefaces of this page are the typefaces of the desktop, with the Papirus icon theme and a dark, steel-blue look throughout.

Genesis Imager

Your password goes on the card.
Never in the image.

A password built into an image is the same on every box, and anyone with the image can read it. Genesis Imager writes your user, password and Wi-Fi onto the card while it flashes, so the box boots straight to the desktop, on the network, with nothing to type on it.

Genesis Imager
Ready
Illustration
  1. 1
    You type it onceUser, password, host name, Wi-Fi, time zone, keyboard, SSH keys. The Imager checks them the way the box will, and refuses a bad value before it writes anything.
  2. 2
    The card gets a hash, not a passwordYour account password is stored as a salted SHA-512 hash with 100,000 rounds, in a small reserved area of the boot partition. The Wi-Fi password has to be usable, so it sits there readable until the first boot.
  3. 3
    It writes, then reads it backThe whole card, partition table last, then read back to check; on Linux and macOS the card is ejected for you. Only removable cards and USB drives are offered, and the disk your computer runs from is never listed.
  4. 4
    First boot applies it, then wipes itIt happens early in the first start, before the network, SSH and the login screen. The settings are then overwritten on the card, and a summary without passwords is left in genesis-setup-result.txt for you.

For developers

Two Pythons for you.
One for the system. Kept apart.

The system's Python runs the desktop, apt and the settings, so it is left exactly as Debian ships it. Yours live on their own, where nothing in the OS depends on them.

yours · default Python 3.10 python pip python3.10 3.10.22, the interpreter VS Code is set to
yours · newer Python 3.15 python3.15 pip3.15 3.15.1, for trying what is new
the system's Python 3.13 python3 Debian's own. apt, the shell and Genesis Settings run on it; pip refuses to install into it

Your Pythons are self-contained builds under /opt/python. pip install without a virtual environment goes to ~/.local. Need another version? uv is installed: uv python install 3.14. Python 3.10 is end-of-life, so plan to move your code on, and some packages have no 64-bit ARM wheels for 3.15 yet.

Remote access

    
The box, in a browser tab.

    
The Acu Connect client is built in. Link a box once, and its terminal and its desktop open in a browser at connect.acutrotech.com.

  


  

    

      
    


    

      

        
link a box

        

      

      
    
        
  • A terminal as the box's user, and the desktop either view-only or with mouse and keyboard
    • 
        
  • Starts at every boot and waits quietly until the box is linked
    • 
        
  • Runs on its own Python with hash-locked packages, which system updates do not touch
    • 
        
  • One server, fixed: it links to connect.acutrotech.com and nowhere else
    • 
        
  • Unlink with sudo acu-connect signout; the client stops within seconds
    • 
      

      

        What it sends
        
Acu Connect is a service of Acutro Technologies and needs an account there. While a box is linked, the client reports its host name, model, serial number, network addresses and load to that server, and looks up the box's approximate location from its IP address once an hour (ACU_NETWORK_LOCATION=0 in /etc/default/acu-connect turns the lookup off). A relayed session passes what is typed and shown through the server, and the server can ask the client to record a terminal session. Nothing is reachable until you link the box, and an image can be built without the client.

      

      
On your own network, SSH and VNC are on as well: ssh nodoai@nodobox.local, or any VNC viewer on nodobox.local:5900.

Built like a product

    
Every image is run from the inside
before it is released.

    
The build does not stop at "it compiled". It writes the finished image through Genesis Imager, runs the first-boot setup and the desktop session inside that image, and takes screenshots. A failed check or a blank desktop fails the build, and nothing is released.

The smoke test runs inside the built image on the build server, with a virtual screen. Starting a real Raspberry Pi is still the last test, and no automated step replaces it.


  

    

      
      
No default password

      
The image has no password in it. You type one into Genesis Imager, or the standard first-boot wizard asks for one.

    

    

      
      
Pinned, not trusted

      
The build's GitHub Actions are pinned to an exact commit. The Python and uv downloads are checked against a SHA-256 kept in the repository, and the remote-access client's packages install only when their hash matches.

    

    

      
      
Security updates, by itself

      
Debian and Raspberry Pi security updates install automatically. The system log is kept across reboots with a size cap, and the screen blanks after ten minutes.

    

    

      
      
Built with pi-gen

      
The same tool that produces the official Raspberry Pi OS images, in GitHub Actions or on your own machine with Docker.

    

    

      
      
You get the evidence

      
A release carries the image, its SHA-256 checksums and the list of installed packages. The screenshots taken inside the image are kept with the build run, and each image records the commit it was built from.

  • Its own SSH host keysEach box generates its SSH host keys on its first start. They are not shared between boxes.

Remote access 16

      
    
        
  • Terminal in a browserOnce a box is linked, a terminal on it opens at connect.acutrotech.com.
    • 
        
  • Desktop in a browserThe same site shows the desktop, view-only or with mouse and keyboard, as pictures about five times a second.
    • 
        
  • Linkingsudo acu-connect signin prints a link and an 8-character code that you approve in the dashboard.

  • Status, unlink, logsacu-connect status, acu-connect signout and journalctl -u acu-connect.

  • Waits until linkedThe service starts at every boot and waits quietly, instead of failing over and over.
    • 
        
  • Its own PythonThe client runs on a separate Python 3.14 under /opt/acu-connect that system updates do not change.
    • 
        
  • Hash-locked packagesIts 14 Python packages are fixed to exact versions and checked by SHA-256 when the image is built.
    • 
        
  • Restarts by itselfIf the client stops it is started again, with pauses of 5 to 60 seconds while it keeps failing.
    • 
        
  • Holds during a renameThe client is not started while the desktop user is waiting to be renamed.
    • 
        
  • Link changes apply at onceLinking again or unlinking takes effect within about a second, with nothing to restart by hand.
    • 
        
  • One fixed serverThe box connects to connect.acutrotech.com and has no setting for another server.
    • 
        
  • Relay modeWhen browser and box cannot reach each other directly, the session goes through the server.
    • 
        
  • Gets throughWebSockets first, ordinary web requests if those do not get through, and a proxy if you set one.
    • 
        
  • Settings filePlain NAME=value lines in /etc/default/acu-connect, read as values and never run as commands.
    • 
        
  • Five sessionsOne box accepts up to five sessions at a time.
    • 
        
  • OptionalBuild with ACU_CONNECT=0 to leave the client out of an image.

  • Built with pi-genThe tool that builds the official Raspberry Pi OS images, with one extra stage of 16 steps.

  • 30 build settingscustom.conf holds 30 settings with their defaults, and an environment variable wins over the file.

  • Add your ownPackages, services, boot settings and files are added by editing a few plain files.

    • 
        
  • First-boot tests99 assertions on what the first-boot script writes and which commands it calls.

  • Unit tests134 tests: shell logic, the remote-access supervisor and Genesis Imager.
    • 
        
  • Imager on three systemsIts tests run on Linux, Windows and macOS, on Python 3.13 and 3.9.
    • 
        
  • Headless desktop previewBefore an image is built, the whole desktop runs with no screen and is photographed. A blank picture fails.

Good to know 10

      
    
        
  • It is a betaReleases are tagged v1.x-Beta. Treat it as a beta until a release drops that word.
    • 
        
  • Tested in the image, not on a PiThe automated tests run inside the built image on the build server. No step starts a real Raspberry Pi.
    • 
        
  • Imager apps are unsignedWindows SmartScreen warns, and on macOS the app opens with right-click, Open, the first time.
    • 
        
  • No on-screen keyboardThe image installs none, and the shell has no touch-specific parts yet.
    • 
        
  • No screen reader set upThere is a magnifier and contrast-checked labels, but no screen reader setup or high-contrast theme.
    • 
        
  • Brightness needs a backlightThe slider and brightness keys work on displays with a backlight. An HDMI monitor has none.
    • 
        
  • Sensor bar shows presenceIt shows which boxes are connected. It does not read or show measurements.
    • 
        
  • Python 3.10 is end-of-lifeIt is the default because existing code needs it. Plan to move on, and expect missing wheels on 3.15.
    • 
        
  • Wi-Fi with a password or noneGenesis Imager has no fields for enterprise Wi-Fi with a user name and certificate.
    • 
        
  • Remote access needs an accountAcu Connect is a third-party service. Without an account there, use SSH and VNC on your network.
    •
